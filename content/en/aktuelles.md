# News

### 03.08.2026: New interactive map for the [list of practitioners](aerzteliste.md "List of practitioners")
As the office keeps receiving enquiries about the nearest physician, a new feature has been implemented: an interactive map for finding doctors nearby. As before, the map contains all physicians and therapists listed so far who are DBG members working on borreliosis and who have consented to their address being published.

---

### 31.03.2026: [New statutes](files/satzung-2026.pdf) and [donations page](spenden.md)
By resolution of the general meeting of 28.03.2026 in Frankfurt, [updated statutes](files/satzung-2026.pdf) apply with immediate effect.

In addition, a separate [donations page](spenden.md) has been set up, offering donations via PayPal and via a [declaration of support](files/dbg-unterstuetzungserklaerung-2026.pdf) (without membership). Updated versions of the [membership application](files/dbg-mitgliedsantrag-2026.pdf) and the [sustaining-member declaration](files/dbg-foerdererklaerung-2026.pdf) are now online as well.

### 01.09.2020: The [list of practitioners](aerzteliste.md) is online
The Deutsche Borreliose-Gesellschaft e.V. is pleased to announce that the [list of practitioners](aerzteliste.md), set up at short notice, is now available online. The list contains both physicians and medical institutions that deal intensively with the treatment of borreliosis. It is still being expanded and is updated continuously.
