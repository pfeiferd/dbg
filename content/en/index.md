# Welcome to the German Borreliosis Society!

[TOC]

The Deutsche Borreliose-Gesellschaft (German Borreliosis Society, *DBG* for short) is an interdisciplinary medical society.

Its members are scientists and physicians who deal with Lyme borreliosis and associated infectious diseases.

Lyme borreliosis is a bacterial infectious disease transmitted primarily by ticks. The name "Lyme borreliosis" goes back to a cluster of joint inflammations in children following tick bites in 1975 in the town of Lyme, Connecticut, USA.

The aims of the DBG are:

- promoting professional exchange between research, hospitals and medical practice
- holding congresses and symposia
- continuing education on the diagnosis and treatment of Lyme borreliosis and associated diseases
- stimulating medical and scientific discourse in order to advance the understanding of the particular features of these diseases and of the difficulties in diagnosing and treating them.

Willy Burgdorfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willy_Burgdorfer), who in 1981 discovered the spirochete named after him, *[Borrelia burgdorferi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borrelia_burgdorferi)*, the pathogen causing [Lyme borreliosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyme_disease), became an honorary member of the DBG in 2009.
