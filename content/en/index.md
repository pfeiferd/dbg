# Welcome to the German Borreliosis Society!

[TOC]

The Deutsche Borreliose-Gesellschaft e.V. (German Borreliosis Society, *DBG* for short) is an interdisciplinary medical society.

Its members are scientists and physicians who deal with Lyme borreliosis and associated infectious diseases.

Lyme borreliosis is a bacterial infectious disease transmitted primarily by ticks. The name "Lyme borreliosis" goes back to a cluster of joint inflammations in children following tick bites in 1975 in the town of Lyme, Connecticut, USA.

The DBG pursues the following aims:

- professional exchange between research, hospitals and medical practice
- comprehensive, up-to-date information and continuing education on the complex and varied clinical picture of Lyme borreliosis
- the advancement of science and research in order to improve the understanding, diagnosis and treatment of this widespread disease.

![Dr. Dr. med. h. c. mult. Willy Burgdorfer](willyburgdorfer-344x457.png "Dr. Dr. med. h. c. mult. Willy Burgdorfer")

Today the German Borreliosis Society has some 200 full members (scientists and physicians).

Among them was its former honorary member [Dr. Dr. med. h. c. mult. Willy Burgdorfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willy_Burgdorfer), who in 1981 discovered the spirochete named after him, *[Borrelia burgdorferi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borrelia_burgdorferi)*, the pathogen causing [Lyme borreliosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyme_disease).

Together with its non-voting members, the German Borreliosis Society currently has around 265 members.
