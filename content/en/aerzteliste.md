# List of physicians and therapists

More and more patients seeking advice and help turn to the Deutsche Borreliose-Gesellschaft e.V. looking for someone to talk to about borreliosis and associated diseases. As a registered association, however, the DBG itself may give neither counselling nor medical advice. Instead, we provide a list of physicians and medical institutions here who are members of the German Borreliosis Society and who work on borreliosis and associated diseases.

This list is updated continuously.

DBG members who would like to be added to the list or who wish to have an entry changed are asked to send an email to the office with the following details:

- name of the contact person
- (name of the institution, if applicable)
- postal address including postcode
- contact details (telephone, email, website)
- fields of specialisation
- billing: statutory health insurance and/or private
