# Donations

If you would like to support the work of the DBG with a donation, we are delighted.

You can donate very easily, without entering into a (supporting) membership:

1. Quickest, online and straightforward via the PayPal donation button – a PayPal account is **not** required. You will be asked for your postal address so that we can issue you a donation receipt.

    <form class="paypal-donate" action="https://www.paypal.com/donate" method="post" target="_top">
    <input type="hidden" name="hosted_button_id" value="BYTA8XRD6BD64">
    <button type="submit"><img src="paypal-logo.svg" alt="PayPal"> Donate</button>
    </form>

2. If you would rather not pass on your address details and do not need a donation receipt, you can also use **[https://paypal.me/deubo](https://paypal.me/deubo "paypal.me/deubo")**.

3. In the traditional way, in writing by direct debit: please download the **[declaration of support](files/dbg-unterstuetzungserklaerung-2026.pdf)** (German), print it, fill it in and send it by post or as a scan by email to the address given in the form.

**Thank you very much for your support!**
